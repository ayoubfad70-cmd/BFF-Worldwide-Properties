Learning that builds skills for work and for life.

BFF Properties is the trading name of BFF Worldwide Properties LLC, an education and training company based in Sheridan, Wyoming. We focus on knowledge, professional development and practical skills advancement.

Our work is built on one idea: people grow when learning is clear, useful and within reach.

About BFF Properties

BFF Worldwide Properties LLC is a United States company in the education and training sector, committed to education and to the professional development of the people we serve.

Mission

To make quality learning and professional development accessible, practical and worthwhile.

Vision

A world where people can build knowledge and skills at every stage of their working lives.

Our core values

KnowledgeLearning grounded in accurate, useful information.
ExcellenceHigh standards in everything we prepare and deliver.
AccessibilityLearning that people can reach and understand.
IntegrityHonest, transparent and responsible conduct.
GrowthProgress for individuals and the organisations they work in.

Our approach

We start with the learner and work toward what they can do afterwards. Four principles guide how we think about education and training.

Learning-focused

Content and structure are shaped around how people actually learn.

Practical knowledge

Ideas are tied to real tasks so learning can be applied straight away.

Continuous improvement

We review and refine what we offer so it keeps getting better.

Professional development

Skills are chosen for their value in careers and workplaces.
