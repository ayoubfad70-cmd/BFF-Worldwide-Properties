BrainForge Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

In short: BrainForge keeps your game progress on your own device. We do not require an account, we do not collect your personal information on our servers, and we do not share your data with anyone.

This policy explains how the BrainForge mobile app (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use it. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information stored on your device

To make the app work, BrainForge saves the following information in the app’s private storage on your device:

  • The nickname you choose (you can leave the default, “Player”).
  • Your game results: scores, accuracy, response times, personal bests, and which games you played and when.
  • Your progress: experience points (XP), level, training streaks, achievements, and Daily Challenge completion.
  • Your settings, such as your preferred difficulty.

This information stays on your device. We do not receive it, and we cannot see it.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, or precise or approximate location.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers and we do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools that send data to us or to third parties.
  • The app does not need an internet connection to work.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, because we do not collect it. The app does not include third-party advertising or tracking services.

Device backups

If you have backup turned on for your Android device, Android or Google may back up the app’s data to your Google account and restore it on a new device. This is a feature of your device and is controlled by your own Google and device settings. It is governed by Google’s privacy policy, not by us.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can change your nickname at any time in the Profile tab.
  • You can delete your progress by opening the Profile tab and choosing “Reset statistics”. This removes your XP, history, streaks, achievements, and daily progress and keeps only your nickname and preferred difficulty.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your Android settings, removes all data the app has stored on your device.

Children’s privacy

BrainForge does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app stores progress only on the device and has no accounts, chat, or online features, no personal information is sent to us. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, please contact us using the email below.

Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage on your device, which other apps cannot normally read. No method of storage is completely secure, and keeping your device protected with a screen lock helps keep your data safe.

Health disclaimer

BrainForge is designed for entertainment and general mental exercise. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new features. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. If a future version of the app collects any new kind of data, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Contact us
